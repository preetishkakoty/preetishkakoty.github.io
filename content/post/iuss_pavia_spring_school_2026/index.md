I attended the [NEXT-GEN Spring School](https://www.iusspavia.it/en/events/next-gen-spring-school-tackling-climate-change-and-natural-hazard-risk-next-generation) at IUSS Pavia, bringing together PhD students and researchers working across earthquakes, floods, droughts, and climate adaptation.

The clearest takeaway: meaningful multi-hazard risk assessment and mitigation cannot be done within a single discipline. The problems are shared although the methods and vocabularies still largely aren't.
