I presented a framework for selecting representative earthquake scenarios, with Wellington, New Zealand as the case study, at the [14th International Conference on Structural Safety and Reliability](https://www.icossar2025.org/) at USC.

Part of the [Selection of Earthquake Scenarios for Wellington](/project/wellington-risk/) project, funded by [Natural Hazards Commission Toka Tū Ake](https://www.naturalhazards.govt.nz/).
