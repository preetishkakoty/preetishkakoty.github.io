I was selected as one of three [UCL Engineering ABSW Media Fellows for 2026](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/engineering/news/2026/jun/2026-absw-media-fellows-announced), through the [Association of British Science Writers Media Fellowship Scheme](https://www.absw.org.uk/pages/media-fellowships).

The fellowship places practising scientists and engineers in media outlets for two to six weeks. I am looking forward to the experience of translating research into science journalism, and to thinking more carefully about how scientists can engage with public audiences at a time when that engagement matters more than ever.
