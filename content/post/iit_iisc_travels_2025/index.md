I gave a talk at IIT Bombay and IISc Bangalore on how earthquake risk models function less as precise predictions and more as heuristics for policy — which makes how modelers communicate uncertainty and limitations just as important as the models themselves. What counts as tolerable risk, ultimately, is a question for stakeholders, not just engineers.

The talk drew on ongoing work on [earthquake risk tolerance for building codes](/project/risk-tolerance-building-codes/), funded by [Natural Hazards Commission Toka Tū Ake](https://www.naturalhazards.govt.nz/).
