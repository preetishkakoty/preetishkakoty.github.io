A busy week at the [18th World Conference on Earthquake Engineering](https://www.wcee2024.it/) in Milan.

I presented on quantifying variability in collapse risk of non-ductile RC shear wall buildings in the [Seismic Resilience of Aging Communities](https://program.wcee2024.it/?mode=session&id=ASR14) session — part of my PhD project on [collapse risk of non-ductile concrete shear wall buildings](/project/non-ductile-concrete/). I also contributed to a paper on earthquake insurance penetration in India, presented by lead author [Pratim Kalita](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pratim-parash-kalita-5b4627b0/).

I co-organized the [Seismic Risk Models for Decision Support](https://d10qmes3r0zm40.cloudfront.net/wcee2024-congress/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/03094601/WCEE2024_SHR-1.pdf) session with Tiegan Hobbs and Robert Chase, which hosted around 40 presentations across multiple slots.
